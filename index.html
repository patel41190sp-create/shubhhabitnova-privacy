Privacy Policy — Shubh HabitNova Last updated: 17 November 2026 Overview Shubh HabitNova is a habit, task and goal tracking app. This policy explains in simple words what the app does with your information. In short: your data stays on your phone and we do not collect it. Your data stays on your phone Everything you enter (habits, tasks, challenges, quit trackers, progress, profile name and settings) is saved only in the private storage of the app on your phone. It is never sent to us or to any server. No internet, no ads, no tracking The app works fully offline. It has no online account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services. We do not collect, share or sell any personal information. Permissions The app asks for the notification permission only, so it can show the habit reminders you turn on. To deliver reminders on time (including after the phone restarts), Android may also list related system permissions such as receiving the boot-completed signal, vibration and exact alarms. These are used only for your reminders. The app does not access your contacts, photos, files, location, camera or microphone. Profiles on your phone You can create more than one profile on the same phone. Profiles are stored locally and are only a way to keep each person's habits separate. They are not online accounts. PIN lock If you set a PIN, it is a simple lock to keep casual viewers out of the app. It is not encryption. Anyone who has full access to your unlocked phone and its files may still be able to reach the app data. Deleting your data You can use Settings > Delete Data to permanently delete a profile's data. Uninstalling the app also removes its data. Because nothing is sent to us, we cannot recover deleted data. Children The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.
